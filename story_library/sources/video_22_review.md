## Primary evidence

[Munich prosecution service, 6 October 2021](https://www.justiz.bayern.de/gerichte-und-behoerden/staatsanwaltschaft/muenchen-1/presse/2021/3.php)

Investigations began in July 2018. The July 2020 investigation group became a special commission in October 2020 following the September operation. The statement describes 37 police suspects and 21 other suspects at that stage. Its important qualification: the accused were not a closed group, and mutual contact or knowledge of every other's conduct cannot be assumed. Digital material comprised about seven million messages, 6.7 million images and over 130,000 videos. These are reviewed files, not counts of criminal acts. Charges in the announcement are allegations, not judgments. It also records discontinued cases, so the suspect count must never become a conviction count.

[Bavarian parliamentary answer dated 1 February 2022, document 18/20013](https://www1.bayern.landtag.de/www/ElanTextAblage_WP18/Drucksachen/Schriftliche%20Anfragen/18_0020013.pdf)

The special commission transferred to the regular organisation on 5 October 2021. The 217 entries refer to offences recorded under specified laws as of 23 December 2021. A separate answer gives 235 including additional legal categories. Neither figure is a final conviction total. Sixteen investigations had been discontinued for insufficient suspicion. Twenty five disciplinary proceedings had been initiated, not twenty five dismissals. Suspending duties and permanent removal from service are different. Criminal and disciplinary outcomes require separate treatment. Retain dates when citing these figures.

[Section 31, German Narcotics Act](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/btmg_1981/BJNR106810981.html)

Cooperation can support mitigation under statutory conditions. Do not describe testimony as an automatic promise of immunity, a guaranteed sentence or automatic witness protection.

[Section 160, Criminal Procedure Code](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stpo/__160.html)

Prosecutors must investigate exculpatory as well as incriminating circumstances. Do not reproduce the source's implication that prosecutors merely accept police files without independent responsibility.

[Section 153a, Criminal Procedure Code](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stpo/__153a.html)

Conditional discontinuance, including a payment condition, must not be counted as a criminal conviction or described as a fine imposed by a conviction.

## Editorial decisions

Remove soundtrack cues, lyrics, sponsor or channel promotions, reconstructed quotations, speculative revenge motive, drug prices and detailed evasion scenes. Preserve the distinction between reported testimony and independently established events. Avoid naming individual officers with unverified mappings between aliases and verdicts.

Do not repeat claims that this was definitively Germany's largest police scandal, that every officer knew, that drugs found in a car alone establish dealing, or that one institution is wholly criminal. Do not carry over unsupported population comparisons about police suicide or mental illness, or categorical claims that leaving policing removes all pension and healthcare rights. Discuss workplace pressure as analysis, not proof of a particular supervisor's guilt.

The separate June 2025 investigation is not evidence of guilt in this case. Omit unless separately verified and clearly distinguished. Describe the club's role without implying that every guest or employee committed an offence. Avoid a final closure date or causal claim about the business without stronger evidence.
