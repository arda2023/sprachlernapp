## Sources and corrections

[FBI case history](https://www.fbi.gov/history/cases-and-criminals/amerithrax-or-anthrax-investigation) records five deaths and seventeen other illnesses, Ivins's death before charges could be filed, and formal closure on 19 February 2010. Attribute the conclusion of sole responsibility to the investigators. No trial or conviction of Ivins occurred. His death is not proof of guilt. Omit suicide method and speculative diagnosis.

[CDC epidemiological investigation](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/8/10/02-0353_article) records twenty two cases, eleven inhalational and eleven cutaneous, with five deaths. Four cutaneous cases were suspected rather than confirmed. Do not describe all twenty two as laboratory confirmed. Illness also occurred outside intended recipient workplaces. Detection, exposure, illness and death must remain distinct, especially for the Senate letters. Omit any assertion that everyone exposed in a Senate office became sick.

[National Research Council review, 2011](https://www.nationalacademies.org/news/science-alone-does-not-establish-source-of-anthrax-used-in-2001-mailings) found the scientific evidence alone insufficient for a definitive origin determination. Genetic similarities were consistent with the FBI account, but did not rule out other sources. The committee evaluated science, not the entirety of the criminal evidence. Do not imply its report cleared Ivins or proved an alternative perpetrator. Omit preparation steps, equipment and optimization information. Do not repeat the transcript's claim that a strain occurred in nature only once, nor treat a strain label as a unique personal identification.

[Federal court record on disclosure, 2008](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-dcd-1_08-mc-00576/pdf/USCOURTS-dcd-1_08-mc-00576-1.pdf) confirms Hatfill was publicly a person of interest, faced searches and intense attention, was definitively ruled out by DOJ and settled his Privacy Act lawsuit without an admission of liability. Avoid an unsupported single test explanation for his exclusion. No need to add a monetary amount.

[CDC historical table](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/9/5/02-0388-t3) documents deliberate contamination in Oregon in 1984. Thus 2001 was not the first biological attack in the United States. Mention the earlier event without operational detail.

[CDC clinical overview](https://www.cdc.gov/anthrax/hcp/antibiotics/index.html) supports a short general account of anthrax as bacterial disease, natural occurrence, animal association and severe illness. It is not spread like a respiratory virus between people. Avoid detailed medical instructions or blanket claims that all forms have the same prognosis. Treatment can help but is not a guarantee.

## Narrative choices

Retain Stevens and Blanco, the media and Senate targets, harm to postal workers and people outside targeted workplaces, public fear after September 11, the long inquiry, Hatfill's exclusion, the official conclusion about Ivins and the independent scientific review. Names and detailed individual scenes not independently confirmed remain documentary accounts or are omitted. Do not invent private thoughts, dialogue or precise exposure moments.

The transcript wrongly conflates al Qaeda with Islamic State. Distinguish the organizations and state only that proximity to September 11 prompted suspicion, not that the attacks were proved connected. Do not reproduce threatening letter text or lyrics. Omit technical manufacture, dissemination, exposure quantities, protective regimens for an attacker and exact contamination sampling details. These are unnecessary for the historical narrative.

Scientific association, access, opportunity and guilt are different propositions. Alleged vaccine program motives remain investigators' theory, not an established inner motive. Do not turn mental health allegations into evidence that a person is dangerous. Closing an investigation is not equivalent to a judicial finding after a defence has tested the evidence.
