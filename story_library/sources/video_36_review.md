## Enforcement and criminal activity

[DOJ Project Python announcement, 11 March 2020](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/dea-led-operation-nets-more-600-arrests-targeting-c-rtel-jalisco-nueva-generaci-n), opened. It reports six months of coordinated activity, more than 600 arrests and 350 indictments. These are not 350 convictions or necessarily 350 distinct charged individuals. Do not fold later arrests of relatives into the same operation. A continuing organization does not prove the operation achieved nothing. Explain the difference between disruption and permanent dismantling.

[DOJ conviction announcement for El Menchito](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/notorious-mexican-cartel-leader-convicted-international-drug-trafficking-and-firearms), primary search excerpt, describes evidence of his order in the 1 May 2015 attack on a Mexican armed forces helicopter, with at least nine deaths. Correct the transcript's generic police helicopter label and do not automatically assign his son's specific orders to El Mencho. Omit weapons mechanics. Further inspect conviction date and full scope if retained.

[FinCEN, OFAC and FBI notice, 16 July 2024](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-ofac-and-fbi-joint-notice-timeshare-fraud-associated-mexico-based), primary search excerpt, documents concern about CJNG associated fraud targeting US owners of timeshares in Mexico. This is not simply tourists being cheated on holiday apartment rentals. Retain only a general attributed example of non drug revenue, without scam scripts or financial concealment procedures.

[DOJ indictment announcement, 22 September 2025](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/senior-cjng-member-indicted-wire-fraud-money-laundering-and-terrorism-charges-operating), primary search excerpt, reports charges against named alleged participants in a timeshare fraud scheme. These are allegations, not convictions. Avoid unrelated individuals if they add little to the story.

[Federal Register FTO notice, 20 February 2025](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-20/pdf/2025-02861.pdf) confirms CJNG's US foreign terrorist designation. Separate this from the simultaneous SDGT designation. The eight named organizations were not all Mexican drug cartels. State the US legal designation with its date, not a universal jurisdictional status or proof that all allegations about every member are true.

## Claims to omit or verify further

The assertions that CJNG killed 75,000 people and caused 80 percent of cartel deaths have no verified denominator or original dataset here. Omit. National homicide totals are not synonymous with cartel attributable deaths. Avoid superlatives such as the world's most powerful or brutal cartel without a defined measure.

The 2011 Veracruz victims must not all be labelled proven Zetas members merely because perpetrators or officials claimed it. If retained, verify the event and describe victims without unsupported criminal affiliation. Remove graphic forced cannibalism testimony and corpse imagery. A brief account of coercion, intimidation and civilian harm can convey the seriousness without sensational detail.

Early biography, schooling, farm employment, US convictions, deportation and police service require reliable sources. The love affair alleged to have caused a cartel war is not established by the sources reviewed and is unnecessary private gossip. Do not invent secret meetings, motives or dialogue. Dates and family ties in this transcript are unreliable.

The history of Mexican trafficking did not begin with cocaine, and routes through the Caribbean mattered before shifts towards Mexico. Avoid claiming nearly every 1980s route already crossed Mexico or that Escobar's death alone transferred the entire market. NAFTA is historical context, not a sufficient causal explanation for illicit expansion. Describe alliances and fragmentation cautiously, not as four perfectly uniform corporations.

The franchise analogy is an analytical description, not proof that every cell pays a fixed royalty or obeys a single CEO. Diversified crime, local partnerships and intimidation can be discussed without production recipes, recruitment targeting instructions, extortion methods or laundering steps. Gifts and social media propaganda do not establish genuine popular consent.

Claims about a December 2024 meeting and an exclusive division of fentanyl versus methamphetamine/cocaine markets require the original Wall Street Journal report and careful attribution. Omit until checked. Do not say a fentanyl market collapsed solely because of US pressure. The source's present succession uncertainty is superseded by Treasury's later assessment.

Do not frame López Obrador's slogan as proof there were no armed operations during his administration, or label all national murders as casualties of one strategy. A leader focused strategy can be discussed as a policy whose effects depend on institutions, replacement leadership and local conflicts. General causality needs stronger evidence than a sequence of events.

Remove insurance and eSIM advertisements, discount codes, animation team promotion and production cues. No reconstruction of private children's farewell, illness speculation, cosmetic appearance or funeral spectacle is needed.

Return to February 2026 death and retaliation, then the later Treasury succession assessment. End with the difference between removing a leader and reducing a network's ability to harm people.
