# Editorial review: video 24

Status: All five adaptations written and mechanically validated. App integration pending.

Actual topic: the railway extortionist known as Monsieur X, active in southwestern Germany in the 1970s. The opening radio scene is a reconstruction, not a story about radio history. Category: Gesellschaft. Aim for about 1,650 to 1,850 English words, retaining the threatening correspondence, interrupted handovers, escalating danger, Strasbourg lead, arrest, circumstantial trial and later denial. Do not turn the narrative into a general forensic textbook.

## Source perspective

[LEO-BW catalogue entry for the prosecutor's book](https://www.leo-bw.de/fr/detail/-/Detail/details/DOKUMENT/wlbblb_labi/1611681960/Der%2BEisenbahnattenta%CC%88ter%2BMonsieur%2BX%2Bvon%2Bd%2BSpur%2Bzum%2BBeweis)

Confirms Reiner Haehling von Lanzenauer's book, Der Eisenbahnattentäter Monsieur X: Von der Spur zum Beweis, published in 1980. The catalogue does not provide the full case file or establish every assertion in the book. The supplied documentary says it relies heavily on that prosecution perspective. Keep that limitation visible when describing the investigation and evidence.

## Historical reporting and limitations

[Contemporary trial reporting, Die Zeit, 16 February 1979](https://www.zeit.de/1979/08/noch-viele-raetsel-um-monsieur-x/seite-2)

The available excerpt supports the arrest on 17 February 1978 and Kraft's denial. Do not infer that arrest itself established guilt. Access was limited to the retrieved excerpt, not a complete court record. This source has a restrictive word limit and should not become the basis for a lengthy reproduced account.

[Contemporary verdict reporting, Die Zeit, 16 March 1979](https://www.zeit.de/1979/12/knallharte-beweise)

The available excerpt confirms the circumstantial character of the prosecution. It reports a casualty figure differing from the supplied transcript. Use people injured, including serious injuries, rather than collapsing the transcript's nineteen seriously injured and the report's twenty five injured into one supposedly settled total. No death is reported in the derailment. Do not present a fresh inspection of the judgment as completed.

[Badische Zeitung, 16 October 2007, scanned newspaper page](https://sdd9a3c45deeeb7bd.jimcontent.com/download/version/1242460474/module/527027009/name/ext1_16.10.2007.pdf)

This is a third party hosted scan of Patrik Müller's original newspaper reporting, not a court document. It describes the 1979 life sentence, the interviewee's continuing denial and a disputed circumstantial case. The article uses Günther Krumm as a substitute name. Do not treat that as a second suspect or expose present residence details. The documentary also interviews Müller about this encounter. Preserve the 2007 date, with no implication that the interview or the person's circumstances are current.

The story may retain Hermann Kraft as the name used in the supplied documentary and historical reporting. Describe him as the man convicted as Monsieur X, while distinguishing that verdict from his continuing denial. Do not infer innocence from denial or certainty about every expert claim from conviction. Exact appellate dates, later escape chronology and precise prison duration are unnecessary and were not independently established from primary records here.

## Forensic explanation

[Bavarian State Criminal Police Office, phonetics](https://www.polizei.bayern.de/kriminalitaet/kriminaltechnik/003767/index.html)

Describes assessment of vocal and linguistic parameters and the weighing of similarities and differences in voice comparison. A comprehensive forensic comparison is not simply an automatic voice match. This contemporary explanation is context, not proof that the same tools were used in the 1979 trial.

[Brandenburg police, speaker examination](https://polizei.brandenburg.de/seite/sprecher-erkennung-identifizierung-von-p/60613)

Distinguishes speaker profiling, voice comparison, transcription and signal enhancement. Preserve the difference between identifying possible regional features and identifying a particular person. Dialect features can support a hypothesis but do not establish a unique biography or an exact individual match on their own.

[Federal Administrative Court, judgment 1 D 2.07, paragraphs 49 to 52](https://ssl.bverwg.de/190608U1D2.07.0)

Explains the importance of appropriate, authenticated comparison texts and expert assessment. In that separate case, a linguistic assessment did not suffice to establish authorship to the required standard. Do not suggest this judgment reconsidered Kraft's conviction. Use it only to ground the general caution that linguistic resemblance and legal proof are not interchangeable.

[Bundestag, government account of KISTE, 2013](https://www.bundestag.de/webarchiv/presse/hib/2013_10/04-260066)

Describes analysis of spelling, grammar and style for authorship comparison. This supports a short general explanation, not a claim that KISTE was used in the historical case. Handwriting examination, typewriter examination, written language comparison and forensic voice comparison are separate methods. Do not combine them into one infallible test.

## Editorial corrections and omissions

Use Deutsche Bundesbahn for the institution in the 1970s, not the later Deutsche Bahn company. Keep the Hecklingen derailment on 17 October 1977 and the extortion beginning in 1975. The initial demand of 100,000 marks and later 250,000 marks may remain as reported in the source. The offer to repay money with interest belongs to the letter writer's presentation, not an independently credible business arrangement.

Omit exact sabotage construction, component counts, dimensions, attachment methods, patrol times, patrol gaps and payment delivery recipes. The narrative needs damaged railway infrastructure and consequences for travellers, not instructions for recreating attacks. Remove reconstructed opening broadcasts, invented telephone dialogue, music cues and song lyrics. No direct quotations are needed.

Retain failed handovers in a compact sequence without making an unsupported claim about the offender's private thoughts. Statements about debts, disability, family and illness were made by the anonymous writer and must not become independently verified biography. Casino visits and financial problems can be reported as parts of the investigation, not as proof that gambling alone caused the attacks.

The Strasbourg hotel lead and recorded vehicle registration are important to the documentary narrative. Do not reproduce the full plate. Attribute the delayed recognition of the lead to the account rather than assert a specific intelligence agency deliberately rejected decisive evidence for a proven reason. Television publicity can reconnect a lead without itself proving guilt.

Avoid the transcript's unqualified assertion of a perfect one to one dialect match, categorical psychological diagnoses from writing and unsupported numerical claims about comparisons or overtime. Do not describe life imprisonment as a fixed number of years. End with the conviction, later denial and the distinction between a legal outcome and the limits of an outside retelling, while keeping injured passengers visible.

Verification remains partial: no original judgment, complete prosecution file or full prosecutor's book was inspected. The adaptation should not claim otherwise.
