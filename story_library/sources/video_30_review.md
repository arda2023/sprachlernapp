An updated page with the same numeric identifier reports the consultation results: [ministry follow-up](https://www.education.gouv.fr/lyceens-etudiants-ce-qui-change-pour-vous-506143). These are future commitments, not completed outcomes. A restrained dated closing update prevents the adaptation from freezing the story at the transcript's final call to action.

Neither supports treating every participant as a rioter.

[Les Surligneurs, published 8 October, updated 22 October](https://lessurligneurs.eu/mobilisation-lyceenne-non-cette-video-ne-prouve-pas-que-bally-bagayoko-a-mis-le-feu-a-une-poubelle/) identifies the burning bin scene as 28 September near the Bartholdi school in Saint-Denis. The transcript's placement after 6 October can mislead readers about chronology. Do not describe dancing as established fact or assert that he started the fire. This review inspected the published comparison and descriptions, not the original complete camera files.

[Contemporary 17 October live reporting](https://www.centrepresseaveyron.fr/2026/10/17/direct-manifestations-du-17-octobre-une-convergence-des-coleres-dans-la-rue-en-france-entre-jeunes-et-gilets-jaunes-a-quoi-sattendre-ce-samedi-13606840.php) attributes 550,000 national participants to authorities and 800,000 to organisers. Other rolling reports contain earlier totals. Avoid false precision and retain attribution if figures are used. Arrest totals change during the reporting period and do not equal convictions.

## Adaptation decisions

Keep the educational starting point, spread through social media, broader cost of living solidarity, disputed police conduct, damage and political argument. Do not replace the entire narrative with a partisan account or imply that violent conduct represents all students, migrants or political supporters.

No independent verification here of the transcript's exact 1,800 schools, 90 percent of universities, every third school, exact statue height, named minors' injuries, weapon classification, alleged intelligence assessment or foreign politicians' full speeches. Omit those specifics rather than presenting them as checked. Different school types, closures, attempted blockades and actual blockades must not be added or conflated.

Reported equipment use or injuries do not establish every participant's intention or legal responsibility. Do not repeat the police union's proposed account of a teenager's intention as fact. No tactical instructions for riots, weapon construction or evasion.

Use September and October 2026 explicitly. The source's 31 October German action is still a future event on the review date. Do not claim it happened. Omit speculative European revolution framing and the phrase France is burning as a factual description of an entire country. Omit song lyrics and production cues. A later update is a dated editorial addition, not a claim that the transcript contained it.
