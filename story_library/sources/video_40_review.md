## Required corrections

- Correct the substance name to N,N-dimethyltryptamine. The opening transcription says diethyltryptamine, which is a different substance. Avoid the unsupported ranking as the world's strongest psychedelic.
- Distinguish DMT from 5-MeO-DMT. Their findings and trial outcomes cannot be interchanged. Omit the filmed toad secretion episode and practical consumption instructions from this educational account.
- Ayahuasca is a variable preparation, not chemically identical to isolated DMT. The vine is not an irrelevant or mistaken collection: its beta-carbolines affect metabolism and pharmacology. Avoid declaring what Spruce's particular cup contained without chemical evidence, or claiming a universal two-ingredient recipe.
- Do not present Spruce as discovering Indigenous knowledge or a precise date of thousands of years of use without evidence. Use nineteenth-century observations and respect existing cultural knowledge. Exact ceremony date and word etymology remain unverified. Search located his 1873 original report, but full PDF fetch failed because of size; do not claim the complete original was read.
- Avoid the transcript's claim that Hofmann's April 19 experiment was the first exposure without addressing his earlier accidental exposure. Verify with primary memoir before retaining dates and details. No dose recreation needed.
- Correct treaty chronology: adopted February 21, 1971, entered into force August 16, 1976. Do not say it was adopted in the mid seventies or that all research became completely impossible everywhere.
- Strassman's published 1994 research establishes supervised human experiments and physiological and subjective effects. It does not establish the detailed Sarah biography, exact sequence of five sessions or spiritual conclusions. If retaining Sarah, explicitly attribute her account to the supplied documentary and paraphrase briefly. Better omit sensitive biographical details not needed for the argument. No direct quotation verified against the book.
- Online entity surveys selected people with encounter experiences. Their percentages cannot estimate prevalence among all users. A vivid subjective conviction is not independent verification of an external being. Avoid causal claims that DMT converts atheists based on retrospective selected surveys.
- Connectivity is a statistical relationship between measured signals, not proof every brain region activates more, new anatomical wiring appears, or the brain burns out and believes it is dying. Interpretive theories must remain theories.
- Rat brain DMT findings and human enzyme transcripts are distinct evidence. They do not demonstrate hallucinogenic DMT release at human birth or death, establish the pineal gland as the necessary source, or prove DMT explains human near-death experiences.
- Similar questionnaire scores for DMT and near-death reports do not establish shared cause. Dream comparisons are analogies and hypotheses, not proof the states are identical.
- No blanket claim that psychedelics are harmless or cannot cause problematic use. Retain psychological distress, cardiovascular effects and possible persistent symptoms without suggesting outcomes are universal. Controlled clinical support cannot be equated with any commercial retreat.
- Update therapeutic discussion with the small February 2026 DMT depression trial. Separate its two-week randomized comparison from subsequent open-label follow-up. No claim of established cure, general safety, regulatory approval, or efficacy for PTSD based on a depression study.

## Primary and official evidence

### Clinical pharmacology and ayahuasca

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297216/

Strassman and Qualls, 1994. Small randomized controlled study in experienced users measured cardiovascular, endocrine and subjective responses. Blood pressure and heart rate increased. Controlled results do not establish unrestricted safety. Abstract read.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297217/

Companion subjective-effects report. Describes rapid onset, vivid perceptual changes, anxiety and euphoria, and short acute duration in the studied protocol. Does not verify the source documentary's named participant narrative. Abstract read.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8731519/

1996 repeated-administration study identified. Title refers to four closely spaced administrations. Do not use it to confirm the documentary's unspecified five-session story. Bibliographic record inspected only.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12660312

Riba et al., human ayahuasca pharmacology. Eighteen experienced volunteers in a controlled study. Preparation combines DMT with MAO-inhibiting beta-carbolines. Pharmacological interaction explains why the vine matters. No preparation quantities or procedural advice to be included.

### Brain measurements and interpretation

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36940333/

Timmermann et al., 2023, Human brain effects of DMT assessed via EEG-fMRI. Bibliographic record verified; detailed methods and result text still need inspection before numerical claims. Use measured functional connectivity cautiously, not a metaphor of all neurons switched on.

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-51974-4
https://raphaelmilliere.com/pdfs/timmermannNeuralCorrelatesDMT2019.pdf

2019 EEG study. Retrieved primary discussion describes lower alpha/beta power, greater signal diversity and emergence of theta/delta oscillations at peak effects. Dream-related interpretation is explicitly speculative. Do not claim ordinary dreaming and DMT are the same condition.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7577737/

Primary travelling-wave analysis describes altered directionality and frequency patterns. Participants had eyes closed. Similarities to visual stimulation do not prove perception of an external alternate world.

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-45812-w
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6597727/

Dean et al., 2019. Human and rat enzyme transcript investigation, but direct extracellular DMT measurements and cardiac-arrest findings in rats. DMT persisted without the pineal gland. Human functional implications remain unresolved. Keep the adaptation summary short and species-specific.

https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01424/full

2018 near-death comparison. Thirteen healthy volunteers, fixed order with placebo first, single blind. Questionnaire overlap, not evidence that endogenous DMT causes near-death experiences. Original limitations include order effects, selection differences and contextual factors.

### Reported entities

https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/0269881120916143

Davis et al., 2020. 2561 respondents were asked about their most memorable encounter with an apparently autonomous entity after DMT. This is an encounter-selected retrospective online sample, not a random sample of all users. No population prevalence inference.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8716686

Original naturalistic field-study report located. Do not merge its sample with the larger survey or treat either as a controlled test of external beings. Detailed methods still to inspect if retaining its exact counts.

### Current treatment evidence

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04154-z
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-04154-z.pdf

Published February 16, 2026. Phase IIa randomized placebo-controlled depression study, 34 participants, psychological support. Greater symptom reduction at two weeks in the active group. Subsequent follow-up involved an open-label phase. No serious adverse events in this small sample does not rule out rare harms or establish general safety. Search returned original abstract and discussion; direct HTML fetch failed on publisher authentication redirect. Full limitations and conflict disclosures still to inspect through PDF or alternate primary repository. Keep claims provisional and do not describe this as an approval.

A separate October 2026 JAMA trial found in search concerns mebufotenin, a different substance. Do not substitute it for N,N-DMT evidence.
