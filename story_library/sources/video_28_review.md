## Narrative corrections

The transcript's eight lumbar vertebrae claim is anatomically wrong. The police account instead describes fractures involving both femoral necks and two thoracic vertebrae, with further severe complications. Use only severe injuries with lasting consequences in the learning story, without graphic medical detail or treatment recommendations.

Do not reproduce electrical circuitry, activation conditions, voltages, restraint setup, concealment design, ransom collection escape mechanics or laundering instructions. These are not needed to explain the crime or its consequences. Preserve confinement, fear, injury, ransom demand, release and subsequent investigation at narrative level.

The precise initial recognition by an ex wife, six notes exchanged in Austria and a single subsequent note should not be treated as the definitive chronology. Primary police history instead describes several independent voice tips after a later public appeal and several registered notes as part of a larger evidentiary account. Describe the combination of clues without suggesting voice recognition is unique identification.

Avoid conflating a claimed persona speaking to the captive with a separately established offender. Voices and sounds heard under captivity do not determine the number of participants. State that Zlof later claimed sole responsibility but that this does not settle every question about participation. Do not identify an uncharged other person as a kidnapper.

The 1980 fifteen year sentence was not the maximum punishment for every German crime. Simply give the sentence. Do not imply that a confession was legally required before conviction. The 1997 money recovery added concrete evidence and public reassurance, but the conviction already existed.

Reports differ on the chronology of admissions. Some reporting describes an admission concerning possession of the money in 1996, while the police retrospective associates acknowledgment of the crime with later incontrovertible evidence. Avoid saying that the first confession definitely occurred only after the 1997 arrest. State that he maintained his innocence for years and later acknowledged responsibility.

Do not reproduce the typo referring to a money handling associate as Richard's accomplice. Any later association discussed belongs to Zlof's handling of the ransom, not to the victim. Avoid current locations or occupations of Zlof without current verification. Omit invented private thoughts and reconstructed dialogue.
